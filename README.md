# FINANCIAL — Cash Flow Planning Tool 📊

A lightweight, front-end web application designed to help you easily manage your monthly cash flow, credit card bills, loans, and savings goals. Through an intuitive interface, you can quickly assess your financial health and map out your repayment and savings plans.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [https://mewomuguo.github.io/financial.io/](https://mewomuguo.github.io/financial.io/)

## 🔒 Privacy Policy (No Data Collection)

**This tool is a 100% client-side front-end web application.** I highly value your privacy. **This website absolutely DOES NOT collect, track, store, or upload any of your personal or financial data to any server.** All numbers and calculations you input are processed locally within your current browser session. Once you close the tab or clear your browser data, everything disappears. Please feel safe using it.

## ✨ Features

* 💰 **Income & Cash Management:** Add multiple income sources and set your currently available cash on hand.
* 💳 **Credit Card Setup:** Log multiple credit cards and estimate your new monthly credit card spending.
* 🏠 **Fixed Expenses:** Easily list and sum up your regular monthly living expenses.
* 🏦 **Loan Tracking:** Record your remaining loan principal, monthly payment, deduction date, and estimated payoff date.
* 🎯 **Goal Setting:** Customize your planning period (in months), target credit card balance, and monthly savings goal.
* 📊 **One-Click Report Generation:** Automatically calculate and generate a personalized cash flow report based on your inputs.
* 📥 **Flexible Export Options:** Export your financial report as a **Markdown (.md)** or **PDF (.pdf)** file for personal archiving or printing.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

* **HTML5 / CSS3**: For a responsive and clean modern UI.
* **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Handles all frontend logic, dynamic form rendering, and financial calculations.
* **[html2pdf.js](https://ekoopmans.github.io/html2pdf.js/)**: Used for seamless rendering and exporting of the web report into a PDF file.

# FINANCIAL — 現金流規劃工具 📊

這是一個協助您輕鬆管理每月現金流、信用卡帳單、貸款以及儲蓄目標的輕量級網頁端實用工具。透過直覺的介面，您可以快速檢視自己的財務健康狀況，並制定未來的還款與儲蓄計畫。

🌐 **線上使用連結：** [https://mewomuguo.github.io/financial.io/](https://mewomuguo.github.io/financial.io/)

## 🔒 隱私權聲明 (不蒐集個人資料)

**本工具為 100% 純前端網頁應用程式。**

我非常重視您的隱私，**本網站「絕對不會」蒐集、追蹤、儲存或上傳您的任何個人資料與財務數據至任何伺服器。** 所有您輸入的數字與運算過程，皆僅在您當下使用的瀏覽器本地端（Local）進行。關閉網頁或清除瀏覽器資料後，所有數據即會徹底消失，請安心填寫與使用。

## ✨ 功能特色

* 💰 **收入與現金設定：** 彈性新增多筆收入來源，並可設定目前手邊的可用現金。
* 💳 **信用卡管理：** 支援登錄多張信用卡資訊，以及每月預估新增的信用卡消費金額。
* 🏠 **固定支出追蹤：** 輕鬆條列並加總每個月的常態性開銷。
* 🏦 **貸款規劃：** 紀錄目前貸款剩餘本金、每月還款金額、扣款日及預計結清日。
* 🎯 **財務目標設定：** 自訂規劃期數（月份）、目標信用卡欠款總額，以及每月的儲蓄目標。
* 📊 **一鍵產生財務報告：** 彙整所有輸入的數據，自動運算並產生專屬的現金流規劃報告。
* 📥 **靈活匯出格式：** 支援將生成的財務報告一鍵下載為 **Markdown (.md)** 或 **PDF (.pdf)** 檔案，方便您個人留存或列印。

## 🛠 技術棧

* **HTML5 / CSS3**：打造響應式且直覺的現代化使用者介面。
* **JavaScript (ES6+)**：處理所有的前端邏輯、動態表單新增與財務數據運算。
* **[html2pdf.js](https://ekoopmans.github.io/html2pdf.js/)**：用於將網頁財務報告無縫轉換並匯出為 PDF 檔案。
